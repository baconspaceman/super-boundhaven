# Super BoundHaven — design brief

Source: Anthony's supplied project requirements, organized on 2026-09-30. The long-term vision below is not a mandatory launch feature list.

## Identity and platform

The provisional working title is **Super BoundHaven (SBH)**. Keep “Super” in the name; no Bacon or Spaceman public branding. Preliminary name searches are not legal clearance. This is an independent platforming MMO, unrelated to Anthony's other projects.

Browser first; Steam later, with browser/Steam cross-play. Aim for polished, sharp 16-bit visuals in a side-scrolling open world with other players visible and interacting in real time. Social inspirations include MapleStory, PokeMMO, Club Penguin, and WoW. These are references for feel and community, not permission to reuse protected content.

## Movement, challenge, and replayability

Precise SMW-like movement should create a meaningful skill gap. Anthony wants study of SMW movement and recompilation data to understand movement behavior; this does not imply permission to copy Nintendo code, assets, or ROMs. Build an original movement system and document reference provenance.

The game should be challenging, fair, forgiving enough to encourage retries, and highly replayable. Challenge comes chiefly from precision platforming and timing, with selective enemies and puzzle bosses instead of repetitive three-hit boss patterns. Checkpoint, failure, recovery, and accessibility details remain undecided.

Include many original powerups and original creatures/mounts reminiscent of Yoshi in their gameplay role. Do not make Mario mushrooms or copy Nintendo character designs.

Cooperative dungeons must genuinely require coordinated actions: player bounces, switches, timing, and shared execution. The hardest raids approach Kaizo difficulty mixed with Ms. Splosion Man-style cooperation. They should require at least six or eight players; the exact minimum and maximum are unresolved. Do not make one player's solo completion the default interpretation of a cooperative dungeon.

## Levels and community events

Players can upload levels. A weekly featured level is chosen by AI or another selector; editor format, validation, moderation, appeals, and selection criteria are unspecified. Popular familiar levels can be reused for time attacks, races, and speedrun events. A two-week November event was illustrative, not an approved calendar or schedule.

Mirror layouts and variants with a pursuing threat are desired. Evaluate each modifier and combination separately; do not assume every modifier combines safely or fairly. Rules for event eligibility, rankings, gear, rewards, and cheating remain open.

## World palette

Potential regions include:

- Mechanical factory, jungle, starter grassland-like area.
- Underwater region linked to a pirate area, caves, sky, lava.
- Stormy island, haunted region, space, swampy bayou.
- Dessert region with cakes/candy **and a separate sandy desert**.
- Bustling city with shopping and trading.

These themes are available for planning; not all are required at launch.

## Later construction-site/casino update and lore

A later update introduces a construction site run by cats; it later becomes a casino. Activities are unspecified, and no real-money gambling is assumed.

Cat staff speak and then meow, get distracted easily, and mainly care about getting paid. Their boss is a dog raised by cats who believes he is a cat. His only family member died and left him a large fortune in an unnamed world currency. He spent it all building the casino and lives inside, explaining his presence. Character names and currency name are unspecified. This is fictional lore, not a launch requirement.

## Items, builds, and economy

Useful tradeable items, original consumable powerups, and functional equipment/clothing should change stats or abilities, such as a higher jump. Players should develop unique loadouts and build strategies. Equipment adds flair and meaningfully eases difficult content; the base moveset must still make hard content possible, though exceptional top-level skill may be required unequipped.

Fairness must be evaluated alongside ability differences. A skill tree is tentative. Separate normalized/unequipped leaderboards and separate cosmetic/equipment slots are proposals, not confirmed decisions. Direct trading versus offline market listings is unresolved. Currency types, stats, sinks, crafting, trade security, and paid-power policy need decisions.

## Art, secrets, and identity

Include secrets and Easter eggs, with subtle anime, Minecraft, and pop-culture-inspired cosmetics where appropriate. Recommend coherent original art and rights review; recognizable references do not grant permission to reuse protected designs. Visual quality should remain sharp and polished.

## Monetization and community funding ideas

Ideas include microtransactions, optional monthly tiers/contributions, and a community goals page whose goals unlock free features, events, or rewards, with optional supporter names. Cosmetics-only monetization is a recommendation, not a confirmed rule.

The first funding goal was roughly the Steam $100 app fee plus applicable tax. Treat this as Anthony's planning estimate, not a verified current fee or purchase authorization. Hypothetical milestones include a currency gift at 1,000 downloads and an early-player cosmetic/token at one million players. Amounts, terms, definitions, eligibility, and timing are undecided; do not promise them publicly.

Do not import monetization or beta rules from any other project into SBH.

## Implementation status

No implementation located in the searches documented here. A multi-model coding workflow is imagined, but the engine, stack, network architecture, account system, security model, hosting, and MVP scope are not fixed. The recommended first proof is movement, a small shared region, and one coordinated challenge; see `CLAUDE_HANDOFF.md`.
