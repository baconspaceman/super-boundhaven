# Canon register and "Never contradict these"

This register lists every known fact about Super BoundHaven with a status tag and a source. Tags: **[CONFIRMED]** Anthony's own words or answers; **[ACCEPTED-DELEGATED]** Anthony said "I'll let you decide" and the lead adopted a documented recommendation (binding for development, revisable by Anthony any time); **[PROPOSAL]** a suggestion awaiting Anthony; **[OPEN]** unresolved. The authority for decisions is the dated log in `DECISIONS.md`; later entries win.

**Never contradict these (summary).** Originals only, no third-party assets, ROMs, music or mocap. Art north star is the Super Mario World essence with 100 percent original designs; no cubes or blocks as characters. Name is "Super BoundHaven", keep "Super"; no Bacon or Spaceman branding. Challenging but fair; hardest content optional; mounts easy to get, free, permanent, never sold, never behind raids. Mount roster is frog, dinosaur, flying dinosaur, cheetah; no wolf. Raids are exactly eight players; co-op rooms are two to four. Six base inputs (left, right, jump, run, crouch, action). Base moveset must make required content possible. No paid power. No real-money gambling, no loot boxes; casino tokens are non-cashable and never bought. Skill model is both points tree and mastery-by-use with free respecs. Open-source licensing: MIT code, CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 art, music, content and docs, name and logo reserved. Long-term open-ended scope. No dates, prices, reward amounts or launch promises. Quick-chat-only is the interim chat default. Never commit secrets or waitlist data.

**Still needing Anthony (never decide by delegation).** Monetization model and prices, supporter terms, community-goal promises, early-player and download rewards, launch dates, casino mechanics beyond the guardrail, login provider and privacy and age and chat posture, moderation policy and terms of service and takedowns, final title and legal clearance, character and currency names, whether BY-NC-SA stays once monetization is approved.

The register ends with documentation inconsistencies found while writing the bible, each with a recommended resolution.
