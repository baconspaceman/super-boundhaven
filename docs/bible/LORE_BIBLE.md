All lore is fictional. Only a little is canon from Anthony's own words; the rest here is clearly labelled [PROPOSAL] and may be replaced.

**Canon [CONFIRMED] (brief):** a much later update introduces a construction site run by cats, which later becomes a casino. This is far-future story flavor, not a launch requirement. **No real-money gambling**, no loot boxes; any casino tokens are non-cashable and never bought. Casino mechanics beyond that guardrail need Anthony.

**Established motifs [CONFIRMED in art docs]:** the cyan **Bound Shard** crystal collectible (working name), the **spring coil** signature motif (bounce pad, Coil Antenna, Coil Pack, Pogo Shoes, creature crests), and "haven shards" set into stone pillars.

**Proposal layer (this bible):** the haven is a world held together by Bound Shards, a pun on "bound" as leap and "bound" as tied together, which matches the design pillars (movement and togetherness). Regions are places the haven has grown; mounts are the haven's animals who choose to carry travelers; factions are small, friendly guilds. Humor is warm and quirky, never mean. Names for characters and currency are placeholders with a candidate list (section 6); use `<DOG_BOSS>`, `<FOREMAN_CAT>` and `<CURRENCY>` in text until Anthony chooses.

**Rules.** Lore never gates required content. Lore rewards are cosmetic or narrative. Real-world cultures, brands and living people are never used. Pop-culture nods are shape-language only and original (brief).

# 2. Worldview [PROPOSAL]

## 2.1 The haven

The **haven** is a bright, buoyant world where everything bounces back. Long ago, travelers learned that certain crystals, the **Bound Shards**, hold things together: they knit islands to hills, ledges to sky, friends to friends. Where Bound Shards gather, land forms and stays. Each region is a place a shard-cluster settled and a community grew.

* "Bound" means two things in the haven: to leap, and to be tied together. Greetings and sayings use both senses.
* Shards react to bouncing. A coil spring pad is a "shard-tamed" spring: the haven's oldest piece of everyday technology.
* The haven welcomes visitors. Nobody is a chosen hero; everyone arrives, learns to move, and finds their own way. This keeps the open-ended, creator-friendly tone.

## 2.2 Why players are solid and why co-op needs co-op [PROPOSAL framing]

In the haven, travelers who bounce off each other "share a bound" (the stomp-bounce). Old stories say the biggest gaps in the haven can only be crossed by sharing a bound. This is a lore echo of a mechanic that already exists in the sim; it must never be required reading.

## 2.3 Mounts in the haven [PROPOSAL]

The four first mounts are haven animals that offer a ride to anyone friendly: a bouncy frog from the ponds, a sturdy dinosaur from the caves, a sky-going flying dinosaur, and a fast cheetah from the sands. Each home region has a keeper who runs a short, kind trial ("prove you'll be a good traveling partner"). The trial is the friendly questline; it is never a punishment (see `PROGRESSION_AND_CONTENT.md`).

## 2.4 Secrets and Easter eggs [PROPOSAL]

Odd corners hold "echoes": small scenes, jokes and lore snippets left by earlier travelers. Echoes reward curiosity with cosmetics, titles and lore, never required power.

# 3. The cat construction site and casino (late update)

## 3.1 Public teaser (already published, keep consistent)

"One day a construction site appears, run entirely by cats. The foremen talk, then meow, then get distracted by something shiny, and mostly care about getting paid. Their boss is a dog raised by cats who is completely sure he is one of them. He inherited a huge fortune, spent all of it building something enormous, and never left." Followed by: pure fiction and story flavor for a far-off update; real-money gambling is not part of the plan.

## 3.2 Story beats [PROPOSAL]

1. **The site.** A fenced construction site appears near the Neon Bazaar City, with cones, cranes and cat foremen in tiny hard hats. NPC cats give friendly, comically sidetracked instructions: they speak a sentence, then "meow", then wander toward anything shiny.
2. **The pay running joke.** Cats do small jobs only if paid (a shard, a coin, a snack-shaped item). Paying is a light interaction, not a purchase with real money; it uses in-game currency only and is optional flavor.
3. **The boss.** The dog boss is warm, earnest and slightly bewildered that the other cats call him "boss" while he thinks of them as family. He purrs by accident. He is never the villain.
4. **The fortune.** His only relative died and left him a huge pile of the **world currency** (name `<CURRENCY>`, unnamed in canon). He spent it all on building the casino and now lives inside. Money is gone; the building is huge; he is lonely but happy with visitors.
5. **The casino.** The site becomes a bright, cartoon casino. Activities are **skill-based mini-games** using **casino tokens** that are non-cashable, non-purchasable with real money, and never tied to randomized paid rewards. No loot boxes, no slot machines paying anything of real or tradeable value. Exact mini-games are [OPEN] and need Anthony.
6. **Resolution.** Open-ended: the casino can reopen as a community hub; future storylines are Anthony's call.

# 6. Naming rules and candidate names

## 6.1 Rules

1. **Originality.** No names of existing characters, brands, games or real people. Run a quick search before proposing; legal clearance is OPEN for the title itself.
2. **Plain and speakable.** One or two syllables per word where possible; easy to read aloud by voice-to-text users and screen readers; no punctuation tricks.
3. **Cheerful tone.** No grim, edgy or mocking names.
4. **No Bacon or Spaceman** in any public name; never reuse names from Anthony's other projects.
5. **Currency name.** Must be unrelated to real currencies and crypto terms; must not read as a real-money promise; must work in plural.
6. **Cats.** Cat names are tiny and silly; avoid names of famous cats.
7. **Localization-safe.** Avoid puns that only work in one language unless there is a plain alternative (see `TONE_AND_VOICE.md`).
8. **Placeholders in code and docs:** `<CURRENCY>`, `<DOG_BOSS>`, `<FOREMAN_CAT>`, `<CASINO_NAME>` until Anthony decides.

# 7. Writing lore consistently

* Anything not in the confirmed table is a proposal; label it.
* The cats' speech pattern: one clear sentence, then "meow" (and an in-line action such as wandering off), then a repeat of the key instruction if needed.
* The dog boss speaks like a very sincere cat-enthusiast.
* Keep lore short and optional: signs, short NPC lines, item descriptions, echoes. Never a mandatory text wall.
* Lore must never gate required progress, mounts or raid access.
